Recommendations

  1. Repair toilets and bathrooms first. 65.0% of students name them as their top request and 78.6% rate them poor or very poor (mean 1.78 out of 5).
  2. Tell every student where to report. 36 students (30.8%) do not know the official person or office.
  3. Set up a visible complaint system. It is the weakest governance item: 45.3% agree one exists and 26.5% disagree.
  4. Set a repair-time standard. 46.2% wait a month or longer, and 18 students report repairs taking several months or never happening.
  5. Treat electricity as a reliability problem, not a condition problem. It has the best condition rating (mean 3.18), yet 46 students (39.3%) report more than five failures a semester.
  6. Collect more evidence on crowding before setting a cap. 37 students (31.6%) share rooms with five or more, but this survey found no significant link between crowding and dissatisfaction.

Method and limits

A 13-item questionnaire on Google Forms collected 117 responses between 21 and 29 September 2026 from students of University of Ibadan halls of residence. Ratings were coded 1 to 5 (for example, Very poor = 1, Very good = 5); the condition composite is the mean of the seven Q5 facility ratings. Confidence intervals are 95% and use the normal approximation. Free-text level and priority answers were grouped into standard categories.

Limits. One university and one survey window. Answers are self-reported, not physically inspected. The sample is a convenience sample, so participation bias is likely. Groups such as 300L (9), postgraduate (7) and 600L (3) are too small for reliable comparison.
